# Bounded canonical snapshot reads

The independent-host experiment exposed a serial validation bottleneck: a 38,772,257-byte canonical snapshot required approximately 348.684 seconds over the cross-region path, and the post-volume-replacement read timed out before its 120-second deadline. Those original outcomes remain failures or measured observations in the [physical results](PHYSICAL_SOAK_RESULTS_20261027.md).

`ClusterClient.readSnapshot(timeout, window)` is an opt-in read-only pipeline with a window of 1–32. The existing one-argument serial method stays unchanged. The first reply establishes the frozen view token and total length; subsequent requests use distinct correlations and bounded offsets. Replies may arrive out of order. Only active correlations are retained, so retired duplicates cannot displace live replies. Lost read replies are retried with their original correlation and offset after 500 ms.

Every chunk must match its correlation, offset, token, total size and exact expected length. The assembled bytes must match the full SHA-256 token. An expired view discards all pending chunks before restarting; at most four attempts share one absolute deadline. Timeout and interruption retire the entire pending set. The timeout is explicitly a read-only snapshot failure, not an unknown business order. No order, export ACK or business mutation is sent by this path. The server wire format and maximum snapshot size are unchanged.

Qualification includes reordered transfers over 70 chunks, windows 1/2/16/32, dropped replies, negative offers, bounded expiry retries, expiry inside a full window, malformed headers/correlations/offsets/lengths/tokens, corrupt payload digest, invalid settings and preserved interruption. A real three-JVM UDP regression compares original serial reads with windows 1 and 32 after 384 business commands, verifies exact canonical bytes and an unchanged business sequence, and also exercises the existing 128-worker same-key recovery path.

These tests establish bounded transfer correctness, **not a latency improvement**. The next performance comparison must freeze one artifact and business state and measure serial/32/serial/32, retaining each full result hash and absolute duration. A new successful recovery/fresh-trade probe is required before advancing the failed cold-recovery gate; a faster standalone read cannot substitute for it.

The subsequent [same-state native ABAB and restored-trade qualification](SNAPSHOT_RECOVERY_RESULTS_20261027.md) passed on three new Azure VMs. Its 11.1× read-duration improvement and scope are reported separately from business latency.
