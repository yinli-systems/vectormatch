# Bounded business dispatch between closed read banks

This opt-in candidate addresses the recorded queue wait during maintenance read phases. It has no native performance result yet. The existing default remains `CLOSED`; enabling the experiment requires the explicit parent property `-Dvectormatch.retention.readBankDispatch=BOUNDED_TURN` and CONTINUE file-work policy. Hidden JVM-option selections and unknown values are refused. No node argument or replicated wire operation changes.

After every nonfinal archive/event read bank is fully replied, consumed and closed, the coordinator enters READ_YIELD. It permits at most127 new original business commands during one caller turn. The next `step()` closes admission and enters READ_DRAIN. It waits for all original business tickets to reach definite completion before returning to the same read phase and opening another bank. A caller cannot extend the turn by repeatedly submitting after127 commands; unused capacity is discarded at the next step. Final banks retain their existing verification/file transitions.

No business is submitted while a read lease is open. Command/event/history credits remain reserved until the original verified archive and event ACKs release their own prefix. The archived cut is immutable even when later business executes. The writer still forces and fully reads back both prefixes and exact original control intents before destructive ACK. An unresolved business reply holds the read fence, retains its original identity, payload and absolute permit, and grants no new request or read bank. A late reply for that original may resolve it; expiry/revocation does not become a fresh permit.

The common capacity plan reserves three additional phase frames per possible yield turn. A conservative turn bound follows the aggregate archive-chunk/event-page counts,read32 grouping and per-cycle rounding. Both CLOSED and BOUNDED_TURN reserve the same bytes. Existing file/RPC/raw/journal/growth/free-space floors remain enforced; unsupported rates remain refused.

The independent Python audit binds explicit configuration, native registration, summary and trace metadata. It checks closed read leases and consumed credits at every yield,counts every raw logical admission against the127-command cap,requires original tickets drained before resuming the exact preceding read phase,and binds any publication during READ_DRAIN to its own earlier READ_YIELD interval. Legacy traces cannot acquire the new admission permission merely by adding a phase name. Forged open leases,pending credits,resume phases,128th admissions,counts or missing policy registration are refused.
